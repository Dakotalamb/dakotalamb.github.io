About the creator

A long-running variety creator with something to say.

Box_Beard is a gaming creator, livestreamer, reviewer, and co-founder of Forged Destiny Gaming. His public channel spans thousands of uploads covering games, reviews, occasional current events, and other topics that interest him. His content is conversational and personality-led, giving viewers the feeling that they are spending time with a real person rather than watching a manufactured persona.

Outside gaming, Box has publicly shared his interest in anime, particularly One Piece. He brings that same enthusiasm for characters, stories, and strong opinions into discussions with his audience and collaborations with other creators.
