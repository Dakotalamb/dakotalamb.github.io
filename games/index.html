Tri Ursa

Tri Ursa is a long-term action-horror project inspired by Resident Evil, Outlast, and Dead Space. The game follows a skilled camper who must use a variety of equipment to face a strange zombie outbreak at a water treatment plant. The project is currently in the early stages of concept art, with development updates to be shared as its direction continues to take shape.

A prepared survivor Use the skills and equipment of an experienced camper to face an unfamiliar threat.
An industrial horror setting Investigate a strange zombie outbreak inside a water treatment plant.
A distinct approach to action horror Inspired by established horror games while developing an identity and direction of its own.
