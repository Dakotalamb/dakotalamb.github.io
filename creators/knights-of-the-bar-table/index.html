About the creator

Questionable decisions. Excellent company.

When Knight is not on the clock swinging tools for a living, he is either deep in a D&D campaign plotting his party’s next questionable decision or working toward 100% completion in a game.

If neither of those is happening, you will probably find him neck-deep in a 2/10 Steam release that nobody asked for. If it has bad reviews and a weird premise, he has likely already put a solid ten hours into it. He is also a proud hoarder of older games and consoles. His shelf is a museum, but his wallet is empty and sad.
